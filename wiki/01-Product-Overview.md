# Product Overview & Humanitarian Vision

HEMA is an open-source blood donor platform concept built to demonstrate human-centric software engineering and rapid donor discovery workflows.

---

## 🎯 Mission Statement

To demonstrate how open-source technology can eliminate avoidable delays in blood matching by providing an accessible, transparent, and barrier-free interface connecting ready donors with recipients and healthcare coordinators.

---

## 👥 Core Personas

### 1. Emergency Recipient / Family Coordinator
- **Context**: Seeking compatible blood units for surgery, trauma, or scheduled medical treatment.
- **Pain Points**: Outdated contact directories, unresponsive phone lists, confusing blood group compatibility rules.
- **HEMA Solution**: Interactive demonstration interface with client-side filtering by blood type and city, displaying simulated donor profiles with simulated availability and mock request dispatch.

### 2. Volunteer Blood Donor
- **Context**: Community member willing to donate blood voluntarily.
- **Pain Points**: Unwanted solicitation calls, invasive privacy leaks, unclear scheduling.
- **HEMA Solution**: Self-service demonstration registration form, intuitive availability badges, and privacy-respecting client cards that omit personal phone and email details from display.

### 3. Open Source Contributor
- **Context**: Developer or designer seeking to contribute to a meaningful humanitarian software project.
- **Pain Points**: Overly complex setup requirements, heavy local databases, unreviewed PRs.
- **HEMA Solution**: Modular React 19 codebase, zero-database local setup, single-file beginner micro-issues, and guaranteed 48-hour maintainer reviews.
