# Product Overview & Humanitarian Vision

HEMA is an open-source, decentralized blood donor connection platform built to accelerate emergency transfusion responses through human-centric software engineering.

---

## 🎯 Mission Statement

To eliminate avoidable loss of life caused by blood shortages by providing an instant, transparent, and barrier-free network connecting ready blood donors directly with recipients and healthcare coordinators.

---

## 👥 Core Personas

### 1. Emergency Recipient / Family Coordinator
- **Context**: In urgent need of compatible blood units for surgery, trauma, or ongoing medical treatment.
- **Pain Points**: Outdated telephone lists, unresponsive numbers, confusing blood group compatibility rules.
- **HEMA Solution**: Real-time filtering by blood type and city with immediate live availability status and one-click request dispatch.

### 2. Volunteer Blood Donor
- **Context**: Altruistic community member willing to donate blood voluntarily.
- **Pain Points**: Spam calls, invasive privacy leaks, unclear scheduling.
- **HEMA Solution**: Self-service registration, simple availability toggle, and contact details masked until a confirmed emergency request.

### 3. Open Source Contributor
- **Context**: Developer or designer seeking to contribute to a meaningful humanitarian software project.
- **Pain Points**: Overly complex setup requirements, heavy local databases, unreviewed PRs.
- **HEMA Solution**: Modular React 19 codebase, zero-database local setup, single-file beginner micro-issues, and guaranteed 48-hour maintainer reviews.
