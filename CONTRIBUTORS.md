## 🚀 How to Get Inducted Into the Hall of Fame

Getting your name etched into the HEMA Hall of Fame is simple and welcoming:

1. **Pick an Open Issue**: Browse our curated [**Good First Issues**](https://github.com/TheVicky1/Hema/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22) or [**Help Wanted**](https://github.com/TheVicky1/Hema/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22help+wanted%22) catalog.
2. **Claim Your Task**: Drop a comment on the issue (e.g. *"I would love to work on this!"*) so our maintainers can assign it to you.
3. **Fork & Branch**: Fork the repo and create a descriptive feature branch (`git checkout -b feat/your-feature-name`).
4. **Implement & Test**: Write clean code, verify locally with `npm run build` and `npm run lint`.
5. **Open a Pull Request**: Submit your PR with a clear summary linking the issue.
6. **Celebrate Your Merge**: Once approved and merged, you will be permanently recognized here!

## 📜 Community Code of Conduct Pledge

We are committed to making participation in HEMA a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of level of experience, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

- **Be Respectful**: Treat fellow contributors with kindness, empathy, and constructive feedback.
- **Focus on the Mission**: We are building software that saves lives in medical emergencies.
- **Support Newcomers**: Welcome first-time developers and guide them with patience.
